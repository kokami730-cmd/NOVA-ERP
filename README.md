# 🚀 NOVA ERP

**No server. No SQL. No Node.js. No database overhead — a full-featured browser-based ERP powered by the GitHub API.**

Designed for large enterprises, SMEs, retail stores, restaurants, and temporary or pop-up operations, **NOVA ERP** brings core operational modules together in one lightweight workspace. Skip complex backend setup, launch fast, and keep your data and configuration under your own control.

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

* **Zero Backend Setup**: Run without self-hosted servers, databases, or Node.js. Data storage and synchronization are powered by the **GitHub API**, cutting infrastructure complexity and hosting overhead.
* **Built for Diverse Business Models**: Supports standard commercial workflows, sales tracking, restaurant table status, and temporary or pop-up business operations.
* **Lightweight Frontend Stack**: Built with **HTML, JavaScript, and JSON** for fast deployment, clear structure, and straightforward customization.
* **End-to-End Activity Visibility**: Tracks attendance, claims, payroll, and system-wide audit logs so key operational changes remain accountable.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Point of Sale & Floor Operations
1. **POS**: Checkout interface supporting **Cash, QR, Card, Wallet, Installments**, and **account/credit bookkeeping (tab/ledger)**.
2. **Table**: Tailored for dining and hospitality—**green** indicates an available table, and **red** indicates an active order or occupied table.
3. **Station Intel**: Configure multiple checkout stations/counters and select the active station directly in POS during checkout.
4. **Receipt Viewer**: Look up past transactions and **reprint receipts** for customers on demand.

### Sales, Customers & Promotions
5. **Sales Performance**: Built for sales teams to track **sales targets** against **current sales performance**.
6. **Sales Records**: Centralize and review historical sales transactions for easy lookup and reconciliation.
7. **Commission Report**: Designed for sales operations to view **commission** figures per staff member.
8. **Members**: Create customer member profiles with support for **bookings/reservations, loyalty points**, and **membership tiers**.
9. **Promoter**: Built for businesses involving cross-company or on-site promotions—manage external promoters visiting your business or assign your staff to promote at partner/other businesses.

### Products, Inventory & Supply Chain
10. **Products**: Supports retail, food & beverage, and any product-selling business, with a `More` section housing extended product management options.
11. **Expiry Tracker**: Helps track perishable or dated stock by flagging **products nearing expiration** so you don't miss key dates.
12. **Stock Check**: Compare physical counts against warehouse records to spot **surplus** or **shortage** items (requires manual execution).
13. **Orders**: Supports placing replenishment orders and handling customer orders (requires manual execution).
14. **Purchase**: Create purchase entries quickly for business procurement (requires manual execution).
15. **Delivery**: Dispatch goods to affiliated companies or partner businesses.
16. **Exchange**: Handle defective or damaged goods by processing exchanges with the original supplier/source.
17. **Returns**: Return excess inventory back to the supplier/source when overstocked.

### People, HR & Administration
18. **Staff**: Onboard new team members and create login accounts so they can access the system.
19. **Staff Manual**: Allows managers to publish guidelines/notes while staff acknowledge completion by checking a confirmation box.
20. **Payroll**: Focused on recording employee salary and pay records.
21. **Claims**: Log reimbursement records when staff pay out-of-pocket for approved purchases or expenses and submit a claim.
22. **Timeline**: Tracks clock-in and clock-out attendance across the organization, recording **daily start and end work hours**.

### Documents, Audit Trail, Organization & Settings
23. **Drive Storage**: Upload files, **create and edit documents online**, and download files to your device.
24. **Notes**: Allows staff to create, edit, and delete working notes or logs.
25. **Logs**: System-wide audit trail that records **edits, updates, and deletions**, regardless of who performed the action.
26. **Company**: Restricted to management creation; supports managing your main company as well as **branch companies**.
27. **Login & Data Clone / Home / Setup / Settings**: Includes authentication with data cloning, the home dashboard, initial system setup screen, and core configuration options.

## 🛠️ Quick Start

1. Download or clone this repository (`v3.1.40.3`).
2. Open the **Setup / Installer** screen and configure your **GitHub API** details.
3. Sign in, set up your company and staff accounts, and start managing operations.
