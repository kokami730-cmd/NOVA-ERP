🚀 NOVA ERP
No server. No SQL. No Node.js. No database overhead — a full-featured browser-based ERP powered by the GitHub API.
Designed for large enterprises, SMEs, retail stores, restaurants, and temporary or pop-up operations, NOVA ERP brings core operational modules together in one lightweight workspace. Skip complex backend setup, launch fast, and keep your data and configuration under your own control.
✨ Key Highlights
 * Zero Backend Setup: Run without self-hosted servers, databases, or Node.js. Data storage and synchronization are powered by the GitHub API, cutting infrastructure complexity and hosting overhead.
 * Built for Diverse Business Models: Supports standard commercial workflows, sales tracking, restaurant table status, and temporary or pop-up business operations.
 * Lightweight Frontend Stack: Built with HTML, JavaScript, and JSON for fast deployment, clear structure, and straightforward customization.
 * End-to-End Activity Visibility: Tracks attendance, claims, payroll, and system-wide audit logs so key operational changes remain accountable.

📖 Detailed Feature Guide
Point of Sale & Floor Operations
 * POS: Checkout interface supporting Cash, QR, Card, Wallet, Installments, and account/credit bookkeeping (tab/ledger).
 * Table: Tailored for dining and hospitality—green indicates an available table, and red indicates an active order or occupied table.
 * Station Intel: Configure multiple checkout stations/counters and select the active station directly in POS during checkout.
 * Receipt Viewer: Look up past transactions and reprint receipts for customers on demand.
Sales, Customers & Promotions
 * Sales Performance: Built for sales teams to track sales targets against current sales performance.
 * Sales Records: Centralize and review historical sales transactions for easy lookup and reconciliation.
 * Commission Report: Designed for sales operations to view commission figures per staff member.
 * Members: Create customer member profiles with support for bookings/reservations, loyalty points, and membership tiers.
 * Promoter: Built for businesses involving cross-company or on-site promotions—manage external promoters visiting your business or assign your staff to promote at partner/other businesses.
Products, Inventory & Supply Chain
 * Products: Supports retail, food & beverage, and any product-selling business, with a More section housing extended product management options.
 * Expiry Tracker: Helps track perishable or dated stock by flagging products nearing expiration so you don't miss key dates.
 * Stock Check: Compare physical counts against warehouse records to spot surplus or shortage items (requires manual execution).
 * Orders: Supports placing replenishment orders and handling customer orders (requires manual execution).
 * Purchase: Create purchase entries quickly for business procurement (requires manual execution).
 * Delivery: Dispatch goods to affiliated companies or partner businesses.
 * Exchange: Handle defective or damaged goods by processing exchanges with the original supplier/source.
 * Returns: Return excess inventory back to the supplier/source when overstocked.
People, HR & Administration
 * Staff: Onboard new team members and create login accounts so they can access the system.
 * Staff Manual: Allows managers to publish guidelines/notes while staff acknowledge completion by checking a confirmation box.
 * Payroll: Focused on recording employee salary and pay records.
 * Claims: Log reimbursement records when staff pay out-of-pocket for approved purchases or expenses and submit a claim.
 * Timeline: Tracks clock-in and clock-out attendance across the organization, recording daily start and end work hours.
Documents, Audit Trail, Organization & Settings
 * Drive Storage: Upload files, create and edit documents online, and download files to your device.
 * Notes: Allows staff to create, edit, and delete working notes or logs.
 * Logs: System-wide audit trail that records edits, updates, and deletions, regardless of who performed the action.
 * Company: Restricted to management creation; supports managing your main company as well as branch companies.
 * Login & Data Clone / Home / Setup / Settings: Includes authentication with data cloning, the home dashboard, initial system setup screen, and core configuration options.
🛠️ Quick Start
 * Download or clone this repository (v3.1.40.3).
 * Open the Setup / Installer screen and configure your GitHub API details.
 * Sign in, set up your company and staff accounts, and start managing operations.

📖 功能详细介绍
<details open>
<summary><strong>💳 一、前台收银与门店运营（POS / Table / Station Intel / Receipt Viewer）</strong></summary>
<br />
 * POS（收银台）：支持多种收款与结算方式，涵盖现金、QR、银行卡、电子钱包（Wallet）、分期付款以及记账（赊账/挂账）。
 * Table（桌台状态）：专为餐饮场景打造，直观区分桌台状态——🟢 绿色代表空座，🔴 红色代表已下单或有人使用。
 * Station Intel（收银柜台）：可创建多个收银台/站点，并在 POS 界面中切换对应柜台进行收银。
 * Receipt Viewer（收据查看器）：支持随时查询并重新打印收据给顾客。
</details>
<details open>
<summary><strong>📈 二、销售、客户与推广管理（Sales / Members / Promoter）</strong></summary>
<br />
 * Sales Performance（销售绩效）：面向销售团队，展示员工销售目标与当前实际销售额对比。
 * Sales Records（销售记录）：集中查看所有历史销售明细，方便核对与追踪。
 * Commission Report（佣金报表）：面向销售业务，清晰查看每位员工对应的佣金（Commission）数据。
 * Members（会员系统）：为顾客建立会员档案，支持预约登记、会员积分与会员等级管理。
 * Promoter（促销员管理）：适用于涉及驻场或跨店推广的企业，管理外部促销人员来访推广，或安排内部员工前往合作企业/门店推广。
</details>
<details open>
<summary><strong>📦 三、商品、库存与供应链（Products / Expiry / Stock / Supply Chain）</strong></summary>
<br />
 * Products（商品/SKU 管理）：适用于零售、餐饮等所有售卖型业务，内置 More 扩展选项，提供更丰富的商品管理能力。
 * Expiry Tracker（效期追踪）：自动帮你盯紧临期商品，提前标注即将过期的产品，降低损耗风险。
 * Stock Check（库存盘点）：快速对比盘点结果，找出多出或少于仓库账面的货品（需手动执行盘点操作）。
 * Orders（订单管理）：支持企业内部进货下单，也可协助处理顾客订单（需手动操作）。
 * Purchase（采购开单）：帮助企业快速创建采购单据（需手动操作）。
 * Delivery（配送/调货）：将货物配送至旗下关联企业或合作企业。
 * Exchange（换货）：当采购货物出现瑕疵或损坏时，支持向原供货方发起换货处理。
 * Returns（退货）：当进货过多时，支持向供货来源办理多余商品退回。
</details>
<details open>
<summary><strong>👥 四、团队、人事与行政（Staff / Manual / Payroll / Claims / Timeline）</strong></summary>
<br />
 * Staff（员工管理）：新增员工时快速建档，并为员工创建专属登录账号。
 * Staff Manual（员工手册）：仅限管理人员发布说明/须知，员工阅读后勾选确认，即表示已完成阅读。
 * Payroll（薪资记录）：主打记录员工工资发放与薪资明细。
 * Claims（费用报销）：员工垫付采购或产生可报销费用后提交索赔，可为其开立报销记录。
 * Timeline（考勤时间线）：覆盖全企业员工打卡记录，清晰呈现每天的上班时间到下班时间。
</details>
<details open>
<summary><strong>🗂️ 五、文档、日志、公司与系统配置（Drive / Notes / Logs / Company / 基础模块）</strong></summary>
<br />
 * Drive Storage（网盘存储）：支持上传资料、在线编辑与在线创建文件，也可随时下载到本地设备。
 * Notes（工作笔记）：员工可在其中创建、编辑或删除个人/工作日志与便签。
 * Logs（系统日志）：记录全系统关键变动——无论是谁进行了修改、编辑还是删除，都会留下操作记录。
 * Company（公司管理）：仅限管理人员创建，支持维护主公司以及旗下分公司信息。
 * 基础登录与数据克隆 / 首页仪表盘 / 安装引导界面 / Settings（基础设置）：提供完整的账号登录与数据克隆能力、初始安装引导、系统首页入口以及基础参数配置。
</details>
